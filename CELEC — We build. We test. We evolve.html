EXPLORE THE PROJECT ↓

IT STARTS WITH A CAR.

The first step is simple: build a miniature electric vehicle. But the goal is not just to make a car move. The car is our experimental platform for learning and integrating electrical power, embedded systems, control, sensors and communication.

NOT JUST A CAR.

The real objective is to turn theory into something that works in the real world.

POWER

Understand energy, batteries, BMS, ESC and motor control.

EMBEDDED

Program the ESP32 and build the intelligence of the system.

SENSING

Measure speed, voltage, current and temperature.

CONTROL

Command the vehicle and control its behavior.

COMMUNICATION

Transmit data and commands using wireless communication.

INTEGRATION

Connect everything into one functional system.

BUILD IT. STEP BY STEP.

We are not trying to build everything at once. Build, test, understand, improve, integrate.
